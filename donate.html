iAbout the optional Zeffy tip

We use Zeffy so that 100% of your gift reaches ICPF International, with no processing fees taken out. To keep its service free, Zeffy adds a suggested tip for itself at checkout. This tip is optional and goes to Zeffy, not to ICPF.

To change or remove it:

  1. In the payment summary, find the contribution to Zeffy and open its dropdown.
  2. Choose Other.
  3. Enter 0, or any amount you prefer.
  4. Check that the total matches your gift before you confirm.
