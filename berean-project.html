Bibles for students

The Berean Project.

Join ICPF International in providing English and French Bibles to students in Africa and Asia who do not own a Bible and cannot afford one.

The need

Every student deserves their own Bible.

For many students, owning a Bible is out of reach. A small gift changes that.

Through the Berean Project, your giving places Scripture directly into the hands of students in Africa and Asia, in the language they study in.
