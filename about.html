Our story

Mentoring the next generation.

ICPF is an organization committed to enriching and mentoring the next generation with the Gospel of Jesus Christ.

Who we are

A full Gospel ministry for students worldwide.

We prepare young people for God's purpose in worldwide missions through personal and practical mentoring.

ICPF International is a full Gospel Christian ministry that works mainly with students around the world, with the goal of making them disciples of Jesus Christ.

What began as a student-led movement has grown into a global one. Today we train young people in discipleship, servant leadership, and practical evangelism, and the work has expanded to nations including Cambodia, Vietnam, Laos, Sri Lanka, Nepal, Myanmar, Cameroon, DR Congo, and Uganda.

Mission & vision

Why we exist.

M

Mission

To enrich and mentor the next generation with the Gospel of Jesus Christ, preparing young people for God's purpose in worldwide missions through personal and practical mentoring.

V

Vision

To reach the youths with the Gospel, to disciple them through Word-based training, and to send them in the power of the Spirit to make them effective witnesses for Jesus Christ.

What we do

Three ways we invest in youth.

Discipleship & mentorship

Programs for middle and high school students, grounded in the Word.

Leadership training

Camps that build servant leaders among young men and women.

Camps in the nations

Outreach and practical evangelism across Asia and Africa.
